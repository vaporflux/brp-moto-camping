# BRP Moto Camping — Brand Document

**For Claude Code.** This document specifies the visual identity and gives an exact,
mechanical color mapping for updating the existing site. Everything referenced here
ships alongside this file.

---

## 1. The mark

A handlebar moustache that doubles as a set of motorcycle handlebars, with a floating
circle above the philtrum notch. The circle reads as a headlight over the bars, and as
a nose over a beard. Two shapes total — a circle and one mirrored path.

Design rules, in priority order:

1. **Never add detail.** No outlines, gradients, shadows, or third elements. The mark
   has to survive at 16px and it only does so because it is two solid shapes.
2. **Never separate the circle from the moustache.** The gap between them is the
   composition. Keep the circle centered on the notch.
3. **Never recolor the circle and bars the same.** The two-tone contrast is what makes
   the headlight read as a headlight.
4. **Do not stretch.** Uniform scale only.

Clear space: at least the diameter of the headlight circle on every side.
Minimum size: 16px wide for the tile versions, 24px for the transparent mark.

### 3c. Basemap tiles

The current CARTO `dark_all` basemap is neutral grey-black and will read as muddy
against blue panels. Switch the tile URL to CARTO **`dark_nolabels`** with a CSS
`filter: hue-rotate(-12deg) saturate(1.15)` on the tile pane, or leave `dark_all` if
that filter costs too much on mobile. Judge it visually — this one is a taste call, not
a spec.

### 3d. Add the brand to the page

1. Copy every file in this kit **except** `BRAND.md` and `preview-rounded.png` to the
   repo root, next to `index.html`.
2. Paste `HEAD-SNIPPET.html` into `<head>`, replacing `YOUR-DOMAIN` with the live URL.
3. Put `logo-mark.svg` in the sidebar header, to the left of the title, sized about 34px
   tall. Use the cream version — the header sits on Deep Blue.

## 5. Things that are easy to get wrong

- **`apple-touch-icon.png` is opaque on purpose.** iOS renders transparency as black and
  applies its own rounded corners. Do not add alpha, do not pre-round it.
- **Maskable icons are padded on purpose.** Android crops to a circle, squircle, or
  teardrop depending on the launcher. The mark sits inside the 80% safe zone. Do not
  "fix" the extra whitespace.
- **The manifest `theme_color` must match the page background** (`#0e1a22`) or Android
  shows a seam between the status bar and the page.
- **Favicons cache hard.** An old icon after deploy usually means the browser cache, not
  a broken build. Verify in a private window.
- **Link preview cards cache on the platform side.** iMessage, Slack, and Facebook each
  keep their own copy. Use their debugger/refresh tools rather than redeploying.

## 6. Regenerating

The mark is one circle and one mirrored path, produced by `gen.py` (included). Changing
the moustache curl, the headlight size, or any color is a one-line edit and a re-run —
substantially easier and more reliable than redrawing. Do not attempt to regenerate these
assets with an image model; at 16px the geometry has to be exact.
